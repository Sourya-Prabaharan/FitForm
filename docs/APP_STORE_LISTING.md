## Description

FitForm helps lifters review workout technique from videos they choose to record or upload.

Analyze squats, deadlifts, and bench press sets with computer-vision pose estimation. FitForm tracks joint angles, movement path, stability, rep count, and common form issues such as depth, knee tracking, torso angle, bar-path symmetry, and shoulder stability.

Features:

- Record workout videos directly in the app
- Upload videos from your library
- Review form score, confidence, rep count, and stability
- See detected mistakes and improvement recommendations
- Track previous analyses and progress over time
- Sign in securely and keep your workout history available

Important: FitForm provides general fitness feedback only. It is not medical advice, injury diagnosis, physical therapy, or a replacement for a qualified coach or clinician.
